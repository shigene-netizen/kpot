Privacy Policy

KPOT To Go ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order or sign up for rewards, we may collect your name, phone number, e-mail address, delivery address, and payment details (processed securely by Stripe — we do not store full card numbers). When you enter a giveaway, we collect your e-mail address and entry reference. We also collect basic technical data such as browser type and device information to improve the site.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to process orders, fulfill deliveries, manage loyalty rewards, send order confirmations and receipts, and (only with your consent) send promotional e-mails. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Marketing communications

Marketing e-mail is opt-in and off by default. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing message. Unsubscribing from marketing does not affect your ability to order or to participate in promotions you have already entered.

4. Data sharing

We share information only with service providers necessary to operate our business — payment processing (Stripe), delivery logistics, and e-mail delivery. These providers are bound by confidentiality and may only use your data to perform services for us.

5. Data retention

Order records are retained as required for accounting and legal purposes. Reward and customer data are retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the phone number or address below.

6. Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit and store data on secured servers. While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

7. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Contact KPOT To Go at (678) 335-5388 or visit us at 2955 Cobb Pkwy SE, Suite 830, Atlanta, GA 30339.
