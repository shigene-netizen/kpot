About Us

KPOT Korean BBQ & Hot Pot — To Go

Build-your-own hot pot bowls and Korean BBQ protein bowls, made fresh in Atlanta. Dine in at your table, pick it up, or have it delivered.

Our Story

KPOT Korean BBQ & Hot Pot — To Go was born from a simple idea: the communal, interactive joy of Korean BBQ and hot pot should not be limited to a long sit-down dinner. We wanted to bring the flavors, the sizzle, and the warmth of the KPOT experience to people on the move — whether you are grabbing lunch between meetings, feeding the family on a weeknight, or craving something bold after a long day.

Every bowl is built by you. Pick your broth, your noodle, your protein, your toppings and your sauce — or go for a Korean BBQ protein bowl with white rice, kimchi and grilled vegetables. Nothing is pre-assembled; every order is made fresh when you place it, so what arrives at your table, your counter or your door is exactly what you asked for.

We are proud to be a neighborhood spot in Atlanta — 2955 Cobb Pkwy SE, Suite 830 — open daily from noon, serving the communities of Smyrna, Vinings, Marietta and beyond. Whether you dine in, pick up or get delivery within about two miles, we want KPOT To Go to feel like your place.
